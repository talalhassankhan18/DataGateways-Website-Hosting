# DataGateways Privacy Policy


This is the Privacy Policy of **DataGateways** and its applicable affiliates and subsidiaries (collectively, “DataGateways,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). We are committed to protecting personal data and information entrusted to us by our customers, partners, users, and website visitors.

This Privacy Policy describes how DataGateways handles two broad categories of information:

- **Personal data that our customers and partners ask us to process on their behalf (“Processor Data”).** In these circumstances, DataGateways generally acts as a processor or service provider, while the relevant customer or partner determines the purposes and means for which the information is processed.
- **Personal data that we handle for our own business purposes (“Controller Data”).** In these circumstances, DataGateways may act as a controller or business with respect to that information.

This Privacy Policy includes information specific to Processor Data, information specific to Controller Data, and information relevant to our handling of both categories.

> **Important:** This document is a DataGateways-adapted privacy-policy draft based on the structure and subject matter of the source policy supplied for conversion. Company-specific legal details, service names, addresses, jurisdictions, representatives, retention periods, and contact information should be reviewed and completed by DataGateways before publication.

## 1. Privacy Practices Specific to Processor Data

### a. Types of Processor Data We Collect

We may receive information from or on behalf of our customers and their users. Depending on the services provided, this information may contain different types of personal data.

Examples of information that may constitute Processor Data include:

- Device identifiers, such as IP addresses, device names, device models, manufacturers, operating-system information, firmware versions, metadata, time zones, language settings, MAC addresses, application information, filenames and file paths, usernames, and technical information about computing systems and networks.
- Contact and registration information, including names, usernames, email addresses, telephone numbers, business addresses, and other identifying information.
- Internet, electronic-network, or device-activity information, including system logs, traffic information, URLs, metadata, usage information, and security-related information.
- Information contained in files, activity logs, reports, communications, support requests, technical inquiries, dashboards, portals, or other systems through which our services are provided.
- Other information that identifies or can reasonably be associated with an individual.

Some technical information may constitute personal data depending on the applicable law and the context in which it is processed.

We may also collect Processor Data through cookies and similar automated data-collection technologies, as described in the “Cookies and Similar Automated Data Collection” section below.

### b. Uses of Processor Data

Subject to our contractual obligations and the particular services provided, we may use and disclose Processor Data as follows:

- To provide, operate, maintain, and support our services, including:
  - Providing technical and customer support
  - Maintaining and improving service functionality
  - Providing updates, upgrades, and security improvements
  - Addressing security, reliability, and business-continuity issues
  - Diagnosing and resolving technical problems
  - Analyzing and improving our services
- To enforce the contractual and legal terms governing our services.
- To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, and governmental requests.
- To protect the rights, safety, security, and property of DataGateways, our customers, users, or others.
- For other purposes requested or permitted by our customers or users.

Where our services involve security monitoring, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, or other protective technologies, we may process technical and threat-related information to detect, investigate, prevent, and respond to security incidents and other harmful activity.

### c. Disclosures of Processor Data

Subject to our contractual obligations and applicable law, we may disclose Processor Data:

- To provide and support our services, including to customers, partners, and service providers selected or authorized by the relevant customer.
- To enforce the legal terms governing our services.
- To comply with applicable law or where disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or property.
- In connection with an actual or contemplated merger, acquisition, financing, sale of assets, reorganization, change of control, or similar corporate transaction.
- For other purposes requested or permitted by our customers or users, or as reasonably necessary to operate our business.

For these purposes, we may share information with affiliates, contractors, vendors, and other entities that help us provide the services described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Privacy Practices Specific to Controller Data

### a. Types of Controller Data We Collect

When DataGateways processes personal data for its own business purposes, the information may include:

- **Contact and professional information**, such as name, email address, telephone number, business address, job title, and company name.
- **Online identifiers**, such as IP addresses, account identifiers, device identifiers, and similar technical identifiers.
- **Information about interactions with DataGateways**, including inquiries, support interactions, purchases, registrations, website activity, events, and communications.
- **Business and transaction information**, including information relating to products or services requested or purchased.
- **Training, authentication, or verification information**, where applicable.
- **Audio, video, or photographic information**, where collected in connection with meetings, events, authentication, security, or other legitimate business purposes.
- **Financial information**, such as payment and billing information, where necessary to complete transactions.
- **Website and marketing information**, including preferences, interests, communications preferences, and information collected through cookies and similar technologies.
- **Other business-related information** that we collect directly from individuals, their employers, customers, partners, service providers, or publicly available sources.

We may obtain Business Data directly from individuals or their employers and from third-party sources such as business partners, service providers, payment providers, fraud-prevention services, referrals, and publicly available sources.

Where relevant to security operations, we may also process **Threat Data**, which may include IP addresses, device identifiers, URLs, technical indicators, and other information associated with suspected malicious or harmful activity.

### b. Uses of Controller Data

DataGateways may use Controller Data:

- To provide products, services, support, events, websites, communications, and other business offerings.
- To communicate with customers, prospective customers, partners, suppliers, and other business contacts.
- For marketing and advertising, subject to applicable law and available choices.
- To manage relationships with customers, partners, suppliers, and other business contacts.
- To process orders, payments, billing, and other business transactions.
- To conduct surveys, analytics, and market research.
- For security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, and cybersecurity purposes.
- To analyze, improve, and develop our products, services, systems, and business operations.
- To enforce the legal terms governing our business and online properties.
- To provide security and business continuity.
- To comply with applicable law and protect rights, safety, and property.
- For other purposes requested or permitted by individuals or customers, or as reasonably necessary to operate our business.

### c. Disclosures of Controller Data

Subject to applicable law and our contractual obligations, we may disclose Controller Data:

- For the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.
- In connection with an actual or contemplated merger, acquisition, financing, sale of assets, reorganization, or change of control.
- For other purposes requested or permitted by individuals or customers or reasonably necessary to perform our business.

We may disclose information to:

- Our affiliates.
- Our customers and business partners.
- Service providers and contractors that assist us, including hosting providers, payment processors, marketing providers, analytics providers, customer-support providers, IT providers, data-storage providers, backup providers, and other technical service providers.
- Professional advisers, such as lawyers, accountants, auditors, insurers, and consultants.
- Security researchers and cybersecurity service providers where appropriate.
- Entities involved in dispute resolution.
- Entities involved in significant corporate transactions.
- Governmental, regulatory, or law-enforcement authorities where required or permitted by law.

### d. Legal Bases for Processing Controller Data

Where applicable law requires us to identify a legal basis for processing personal data, our legal bases may include:

- **Legitimate interests:** We may process personal data where necessary for legitimate business interests, provided those interests are not overridden by the rights and freedoms of the affected individuals. These interests may include:
  - Protecting our business, personnel, systems, and property.
  - Providing cybersecurity and preventing fraud or abuse.
  - Providing customer service.
  - Communicating with customers and business contacts.
  - Marketing and business development, where permitted.
  - Analyzing and improving our services and operations.
  - Managing legal and compliance matters.
- **Contractual necessity:** We may process information where necessary to perform a contract with an individual or business or to take steps at the individual's request before entering into a contract.
- **Consent:** Where required by law, we may process personal data based on consent.
- **Legal compliance:** We may process and disclose personal data where necessary to comply with legal obligations.
