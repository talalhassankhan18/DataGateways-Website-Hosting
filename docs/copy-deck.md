## Product detail — DataNerve

- **Eyebrow:** Our Platform — DataNerve
- **Headline:** `Encryption that` + `follows the data`

**Lead (35 words)**

> Most encryption protects data while it sits still and gives up the moment it moves. DataNerve
> keeps protection attached to the data itself, across every hop in every pipeline.

### Tier cards

**Protect** — Encryption that holds wherever the data ends up.

- Encryption in motion, at rest and in use
- Field-level protection, not just whole-volume
- Customer-managed keys, held only by you
- Format-preserving options for legacy systems `[CONFIRM]`

**Control** — Access decided per request, never assumed.

- Policy-based access by role and attribute
- Identity passthrough to the original requester
- Dynamic masking and redaction on read
- Revocation that takes effect immediately

**Prove** — Evidence your auditors will accept.

- Signed, immutable access records
- Lineage tracking across every pipeline hop
- Framework mappings for SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA `[CONFIRM]`
- Exportable evidence packs on demand

## Product detail — AINerve

- **Eyebrow:** Our Platform — AINerve
- **Headline:** `Govern every prompt` + `before it lands`

**Lead (36 words)**

> Your teams are already using models. AINerve sits between them and every provider, inspecting what
> goes out and what comes back, in real time, without anyone changing how they work.

### Tier cards

**Inspect** — See every AI interaction across the organisation.

- Real-time inspection of prompts and responses
- Detection of credentials, PII and source code
- Shadow AI discovery across the network `[CONFIRM]`
- Per-team and per-user usage visibility

**Enforce** — Policy applied in line, not after the fact.

- Redaction before anything reaches a provider
- Blocking on policy violation, fail-closed by default
- Per-team model and provider allow-lists
- Rate and spend limits by department

**Audit** — A record of every interaction, retained on your terms.

- Full prompt and response logging, retained by you
- Attribution to the individual user, not a shared key
- Framework mappings including the EU AI Act `[CONFIRM]`
- Incident replay for post-hoc investigation
